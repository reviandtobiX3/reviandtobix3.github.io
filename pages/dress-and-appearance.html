Dress and Appearance

A support app for JROTC cadets to make uniform standards easier to understand, prepare for, and follow.

Back to portfolio Coming to the Play Store Coming to the App Store

Overview

What the app does

Dress and Appearance has a variety of resources and references to help cadets place each award they earn correctly and wear it with pride. It also provides a streamlined way to access and understand the latest uniform standards.

Status

Open beta

The app is currently being tested and improved through open beta feedback, with the goal of making uniform preparation more approachable and less stressful, and the app is FAR from done so expect many features (and bugs) to come!

Features

Built to reduce stress.

Btw you can click on the images to see them in full size!

Quick standards guide

Clear, streamlined guidance for common appearance expectations. If you want the raw regulations, they're just a tap away.

Inspection prep

A checklist to help cadets prepare for inspections. If you're in a hurry, you can quickly review the essentials.

Every authorised award

A comprehensive database of all authorised awards. If ones missing, contact reviandtobi@gmail.com.

More branches to come

We plan on adding every JROTC branch, CAP, CACC, Young Marines, and maybe more!

But why?

Built for your core values.

JROTC cadets need a faster way to understand standards (especially the new ones), uniform expectations can feel complicated or easy to forget, and I don't know who decided cadet handbooks need one hundred pages but this is the app to help! It focuses on reducing the load and giving cadets a clean reference point they can rely on.

Dress and Appearance is currently in open beta, and feedback is welcome. If you have suggestions or want to report a bug, please reach out to reviandtobi@gmail.com.
