Dress and Appearance Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Dress and Appearance ("the App") is a support tool for JROTC cadets and is currently offered in open beta. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information and what users should reasonably expect from the product.

1. Introduction

The App is designed to minimize data collection and to operate as a lightweight reference tool. This Privacy Policy is intended to reflect that minimal data footprint and the app's educational purpose.

2. Information Collection

The App does not require account creation, login credentials, or any subscription profile to use its core functionality. The App does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device identifiers.

3. Local Device Data

Any preferences, selections, or local configuration choices made within the App are expected to remain on the user's device and are not intended to be transmitted to a remote server or third-party service.

4. Feedback and Support

If a user voluntarily provides feedback, suggestions, or bug reports through the development process, that information may be reviewed to improve the product. Such information is used solely for product improvement and support.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for educational use and does not require personal information from minors. Because the App does not operate as a user-account service and does not collect personal data, it is designed with a conservative privacy posture for student use.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may reference official regulatory material and may include links to external support or funding platforms. Such links are provided for convenience only and are governed by the privacy practices of those third-party services.

7. Security

Because the App is designed to avoid unnecessary data transmission, the primary privacy risk is local device security. Users are encouraged to protect their devices with appropriate passcodes, biometric access, or other standard security measures.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes, feature updates, or legal requirements. Any changes will be reflected in the updated effective date shown above.
